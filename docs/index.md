# PostKaro

**Post your short clips to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and your Facebook Page, from your own laptop.**

PostKaro is a free, open-source app that runs on your own computer. It is the partner of ClipKaro,
which turns long videos into short vertical clips.

## What it does
- **Shows every clip on one page** in your browser, with its title and caption for each platform.
  You can change any of them, remove clips you don't want, and pick a time for each platform.
- **Posts each clip at its time.** It uploads to YouTube (as a Short), to your Instagram account (as
  a Reel) and to your Facebook Page (as a Reel).
- **Checks that every post went live** and shows its link, or explains in plain words what went wrong.
- **Warns you before a login runs out**, and when a clip comes from someone else's video.

## How it uses your accounts
- You connect your accounts once, by logging in on Google's and Meta's own pages.
- PostKaro only posts the clips **you** choose, at the times **you** choose.
- Your logins are kept **only on your computer**, encrypted. Nothing is sent to the makers of PostKaro.

Read the full [privacy policy](privacy.html).

## Get it
The code, the install steps and the guide for connecting your accounts are on GitHub, in the
PostKaro repository.
