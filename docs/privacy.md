# PostKaro privacy policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

PostKaro is a free, open-source app that **runs entirely on your own computer**. It posts the video
clips you choose to your own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page. This page explains
what it accesses, where that is kept, and how to remove it.

## Who runs PostKaro
**You do.** PostKaro has no server, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. The
people who make PostKaro never receive your videos, your account information or your logins.

## What PostKaro accesses, and why

| Service | Access | Used only to |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube (Google) | "Manage your YouTube account" | Upload the clips you choose to your channel; set their title, description and posting time; check that they went live; change or delete a post you cancel before it goes live; show your channel's name. |
| Facebook (Meta) | Your Pages and permission to post on them | Upload the Reels you choose to the Page you choose, and check they went live. |
| Instagram (Meta) | Your Instagram professional account and permission to publish | Upload and publish the Reels you choose, and check they went live. |

PostKaro does **not** read your messages, comments, followers, analytics or other videos. It does not
post anything you did not schedule yourself.

## Where your data is kept
- **On your computer only.** Your logins (the access keys Google and Meta give after you log in) are
  stored in an encrypted file on your computer. The key to that file is kept in your computer's own
  password store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or your Linux keyring).
- **Your posting queue and history** (clip titles, captions, times, post links) are stored in a file on
  your computer.
- **Your videos are sent only to the platform you post them to**, directly from your computer.
- Nothing is shared with the makers of PostKaro or with anyone else.

## How to remove your data
- **Disconnect** an account on PostKaro's ⚙ page. This deletes its login from your computer and asks
  Google or Meta to cancel PostKaro's access.
- **Cancel access yourself:**
  - Google: <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>
  - Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites
- **Delete everything:** uninstall PostKaro and delete its data folder (`~/.local/share/postkaro` on
  Linux, `~/Library/Application Support/postkaro` on macOS, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\postkaro` on Windows).

## YouTube and Google
PostKaro uses the YouTube API Services. By using PostKaro with YouTube you agree to the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is
described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

PostKaro's use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes and questions
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For
questions, open an issue in the PostKaro repository on GitHub.
